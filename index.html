Short Bio

I am a first-year master's student at the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, supervised by Prof. Zhijie Deng. Before SJTU, I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science (Honors Program) from Teli College, Beijing Institute of Technology. My research interests lie in efficient multimodal generation. I am also a research intern at the Deep Learning Lab, Ant Research Institute, where I focus on diffusion language model pretraining and efficient generation.
